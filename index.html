How it works

From “where to?” to “I was there.”

  1. 01

    Tell it where and when

    A place, your dates, a budget in rupees and the kind of trip you like. You get a day-by-day plan with real spots and rough costs.

  2. 02

    Go

    Each day comes with a Google Maps link. Stuck somewhere? Ask the travel guide in the app.

  3. 03

    Take the photo

    At a landmark on your plan, take a picture. We check your location is within 150 m and that the photo really shows the place.

  4. 04

    Collect the stamp

    Verified photos earn points. Points move you up from Rookie to Legend and pay for discounts on stays, trains and food.

Worth the detour

Places your group chat hasn’t heard of.

Pick one and the planner fills in the days, the food and the route.

Arunachal Pradesh

Ziro Valley

Pine hills, rice fields and Apatani villages. Go in late September for the music festival.
Himachal Pradesh

Chandratal Lake

A crescent of blue water at 4,300 m. Camp nearby for a sky full of stars.
Karnataka

Hampi

The ruins of Vijayanagara, scattered across a landscape of giant boulders. Rent a bicycle.

Fair play

No screenshots. You have to be there.

Every photo is checked twice. Your phone’s location has to be within 150 m of the landmark, and the picture has to show that landmark, taken just now.

And go lightly

  • The travel guide suggests trains over flights when the route allows.
  • Homestays and dhabas over chains, so the money stays local.
  • Take only photos. Leave only footprints.
